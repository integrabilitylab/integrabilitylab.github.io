Integrability is an important research area in theoretical physics, with significant applications across many fields of physics. Integrable systems are a central topic in mathematical physics. In recent years, research on integrability has led to a series of important advances in areas including gauge/gravity duality, strongly correlated electron systems, quantum computing, and cold-atom physics. To promote academic exchange in integrability research in China, strengthen collaboration among researchers, and foster interdisciplinary connections across these directions, we launched the “Integrability: From Mathematics to Experiment” workshop series in 2023. This year's workshop is hosted by the School of Physics and the Shing-Tung Yau Center at Southeast University and will take place from November 6 to 9, 2026. Registration will take place on the afternoon of November 6, and participants will depart on the morning of November 9. We warmly welcome experts and scholars to attend.

- **Dates:** November 6–9, 2026 (registration on the afternoon of November 6; departure on the morning of November 9)
- **Venue:** Basement Lecture Hall, Chien-Shiung Wu Memorial Hall, Sipailou Campus, Southeast University
- **Hosts:** School of Physics and Shing-Tung Yau Center, Southeast University
