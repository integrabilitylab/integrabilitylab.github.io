---
title: "Jue Hou, Jie Gu, and Yunfeng Jiang Make Progress on $T\\overline{T}$ Deformations"
date: 2026-08-21
summary: "Laboratory members Jue Hou and Yunfeng Jiang, in collaboration with Jie Gu, an associate professor at the Shing-Tung Yau Center, have developed a harmonic-analysis framework for the non-perturbative study of $T\\overline{T}$-deformed partition functions."
---

Jue Hou and Yunfeng Jiang, members of the Laboratory of Quantum Integrability, collaborated with Jie Gu, an associate professor at the Shing-Tung Yau Center of Southeast University, on a new study of <span class="math">T<span class="math-overline">T</span></span> deformations in two-dimensional conformal field theory.

Their work, **“Beyond Hagedorn: A Harmonic Approach to <span class="math">T<span class="math-overline">T</span></span> Deformation,”** was published in *Physical Review Letters* on August 21, 2026. The team developed a harmonic-analysis method for a complete non-perturbative computation of the deformed torus partition function, confirmed the Hagedorn singularity, and showed how the partition function can be analytically continued beyond it.
