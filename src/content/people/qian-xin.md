QIAN Xin joined Prof. Yunfeng Jiang's group at Southeast University as a postdoctoral researcher in 2026, after earning a Ph.D. from the University of Copenhagen in 2025 under the supervision of Prof. Charlotte Fløe Kristjansen.

## Research

My research focuses on two closely connected directions:

1. Investigating the integrable structures of N=4 SYM, ABJM theory, and other integrable quantum field theories.
2. Applying integrability techniques to quantum many-body systems out of equilibrium.

Integrability provides exact results for simulations of quantum field theories and quantum many-body systems. These two research directions are deeply intertwined and mutually inspiring.
