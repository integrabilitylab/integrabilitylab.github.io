HE Yang joined Prof. Yunfeng Jiang's group at Southeast University as a Ph.D. student in 2026. He received his master's degree from Lanzhou University in 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Yuxiao Liu.

## Research

My research focuses on applying nonlinear integral equation (NLIE) methods to integrable models. These methods provide an efficient way to compute physical quantities of interest. I also study integrable defects in integrable models, with the aim of developing descriptions of richer and more realistic physical systems.
